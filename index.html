Weekly Rounds

Three casual rounds every week, each run by a volunteer host. Show up, toss in, and get put on a card. Courses rotate, so check the Facebook group for this week's spot and start time.

Mon
March – October

Monday Flip Doubles

Monday evenings · about 5:30 PM

Hosted by Andrew McDonagh

Flip for a random partner and play best-shot doubles. It's the easiest way to meet the local disc golf crowd. The weekly spot is mainly Northshore at Lake Fayetteville, with the occasional change of pace at other NWA courses.

Wed
Year-round

Wacky Wednesday

Wednesday mornings · about 10 AM

Hosted by Mitchell Twombley

A midweek morning round with a different course every week, including Cedar Creek and the brand-new Rabbit's Foot at JB Hunt Park. Great for tournament prep or just getting out of the house.

Sun
Year-round

FDGA Tag Match

Saturdays or Sundays

Hosted by Paul Pequette

The weekly round for FDGA tag holders: play for your tag number, with cash games and an ace pool for tag members. Get a tag for $10.

Times and courses are posted each week in the FDGA Facebook group.

Membership

FDGA Tags

An FDGA tag is your yearly club membership: a numbered bag tag that shows where you rank among Fayetteville's tag holders. Play a tag match, beat other tag holders, and trade up to a lower number.

Weekly Tag Match

Saturdays or Sundays · year-round

Hosted by Paul Pequette

A weekly round for tag holders. The day, course, and start time are posted each week in the FDGA Facebook group. You'll need a tag to play for cash, and you can pick one up at any tag match.

Our Signature Events

Four events anchor the FDGA calendar, from a charity round in the cold to a glow round in the dark.

Feb

FDGA Ice Bowl

Kessler Mountain Regional Park · Fayetteville

Our charity tournament, played on a temporary course FDGA sets up at Kessler Mountain each winter. Entries, mulligans, and raffle tickets raise money for Everhope (formerly the NWA Children's Shelter).

  • 152 players pre-registered in 2026
  • $7,800+ raised for Everhope in 2026, a record
Aug

Hogwild Huck

Creekside DGC, Farmington & Carter Park, West Fork

A three-round, PDGA-sanctioned weekend with pro and amateur divisions, a custom player pack (shirt, hat, Innova disc, and a personalized mini), and trophies. Presented by The Disc Golf Dojo and Innova. Proceeds go to the FDGA general fund, including tee pad work at Walker Park.

2026 event page →
Aug

Northshore Invitational

Northshore DGC · Lake Fayetteville

Fayetteville's longest-running tournament, played every year since 2006. The 20th annual Northshore Invitational in 2026 drew the largest field in the event's history, presented by Innova and Rush Truck Centers.

  • 20 years and counting
  • Since 2006 at Lake Fayetteville
Nov

Darkside of the Disc

Northshore DGC · Lake Fayetteville

Our night round. Volunteers decorate every hole with lights and glow sticks, players vote on the best-decorated hole, and everyone plays in the dark with a glow disc. One round, flex start, with trophies for the top finishers.

Who we are

About the FDGA

Disc golf has been played in Fayetteville since 1993. The Fayetteville Disc Golf Association is the volunteer club that keeps it going: we run tournaments, organize weekly rounds, raise money for course improvements, and turn out for course work days.

FDGA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Event proceeds go back into Fayetteville's courses and community, from new baskets and tee pads to our annual Ice Bowl donation for Everhope.
