Who you'll reach

An Active, Local, Loyal Audience

Disc golf is one of the fastest-growing sports in the country, and Fayetteville has played it since 1993. Our players show up every week, and they support the businesses that support their club.

  • 3,600+members in the FDGA Facebook group
  • 1,000+people reached by a single popular group post
  • ~140weekly rounds a year: Flip Doubles (March–October), plus year-round Wacky Wednesday and Tag Matches
  • 152players pre-registered for the 2026 Ice Bowl
  • 20years of the Northshore Invitational, with its largest field ever in 2026
  • $7,800+raised for Everhope at the 2026 Ice Bowl

FDGA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Sponsorship money goes back into Fayetteville's courses, events, and community.
